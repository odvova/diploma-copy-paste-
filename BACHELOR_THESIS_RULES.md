# Rules for Writing Bachelor's Qualification Work
**Source:** Положення до виконання, оформлення та захисту кваліфікаційної (бакалаврської) роботи  
**Institution:** Lviv Ivan Franko National University, Faculty of Electronics and Computer Technologies  
**Specialty:** 121 Software Engineering

---

## 1. Document Structure (required order)

| Section | Pages |
|---|---|
| Title page (form — Appendix A) | 1 |
| Assignment (form — Appendix B) | 1 |
| Annotation (Ukrainian + English) | 1–2 |
| Table of contents | 1–2 |
| List of abbreviations, symbols, terms | 1 |
| Introduction | 2–3 |
| Problem area analysis (literature review) | 10–12 |
| Theoretical chapters | 10–15 |
| Practical chapters (implementation + results) | 10–15 |
| Conclusions | 1 |
| References list | 1–2 |
| Appendices | as needed |

**Total main body: 50–60 pages.**  
Every structural section (Annotation, Contents, Introduction, Conclusions, References, each Chapter) starts on a **new page**.

---

## 2. Page Setup

- **Paper:** A4 (210×297 mm), white, printed on one side only
- **Font:** Times New Roman, 14 pt
- **Line spacing:** 1.5
- **Lines per page:** 33–35
- **Margins:** top 20 mm · bottom 20 mm · right 10 mm · left 30 mm (binding)
- **Indent (paragraph):** 5 characters, uniform throughout

---

## 3. Language

- Ukrainian (default), or foreign by agreement with the department (noted in topic-approval order)
- Proper names: quoted in original language; Ukrainian translation required in parentheses on **first mention**

---

## 4. Page Numbering

- Arabic numerals, **top-right corner**, no period after the number
- **Continuous** through the entire document
- Title page, Annotation, Table of Contents **count** in the total but **do not display** a number

---

## 5. Headings

| Level | Style |
|---|---|
| Chapter title | Centered · ALL CAPS · **bold** · no period · no underline |
| Subsection (підрозділ) | From indent · First letter capital, rest lowercase · no period · no underline |
| Item / sub-item | From indent · sentence case · no period · no underline |

- At least **one blank line** between a heading and the text before/after it
- Headings of chapters and structural elements must not be placed at the bottom of a page if fewer than **2 lines of body text** follow
- **No hyphenation** in headings
- Headings of two or more sentences are separated by a period

---

## 6. Numbering of Sections

- Chapters: `1`, `2`, `3` — Arabic numerals, **no period**
- Subsections: `1.1`, `1.2` — no period after last digit
- Items: `1.1.1`, `1.1.2`
- Sub-items: `1.1.1.1`, `1.1.1.2`

---

## 7. Content Requirements by Section

### Introduction (2–3 pages)
Must cover:
- Relevance of the work
- Goal
- Object of research
- Subject of research
- Research methods and equipment
- Elements of novelty and application areas
- Forecasted development directions

### Problem Area Analysis / Literature Review
- Max **20% of the main body** volume
- Critically assess prior work, identify unresolved issues, justify your approach
- End with a short summary on why research in this area is necessary

### Theoretical Part
- Justify the choice of theoretical and experimental research methods
- Describe mathematical models, algorithms, and methods used
- Describe experimental methodology, equipment, software, error analysis

### Practical Part
- Provide quantitative and qualitative results with analysis
- Highlight novelty, verify reliability of results
- For applied work: describe practical deployment and recommendations

### Conclusions (1 page)
- State the most important practical results
- Formulate what was solved and its significance
- Highlight author's specific contributions
- Propose directions for further work
- Must be based solely on the main body content

### References List
- Ordered by **appearance in text** OR **alphabetically** (ДСТУ standard)
- Format: **ДСТУ 8302:2015** "Інформація та документація. Бібліографічне посилання"
- Every source in the list **must be cited** in the text
- Every source cited in the text **must appear** in the list

---

## 8. Figures (Illustrations)

- Place **immediately after first mention** or on the **next page**
- All illustrations must have text references
- **Caption format:** `Рисунок 3.2 – Short description` — placed **below** the figure
- Numbering: within each chapter — `Рисунок [chapter].[number]` (e.g., `Рисунок 3.2`)
- May include explanatory text (підрисунковий текст) below the caption
- If a figure doesn't fit on the current page, move it to the next page; the caption stays on the first page and is repeated on each subsequent page as `Рисунок __, аркуш __`
- Non-author illustrations: cite the source and respect copyright

---

## 9. Tables

- Place **immediately after first mention** or on the **next page**
- All tables must have text references
- **Caption format:** `Таблиця 3.2 – Concise title` — placed **above** the table, left-aligned
- Numbering: within each chapter — `Таблиця [chapter].[number]`
- Column headers: start with capital letter; sub-headers lowercase; **no period** at end of headers
- If a table spans multiple pages: repeat the header row; write `Продовження таблиці __` above continuation
- The word `Таблиця __` appears only once (first part); continuation pages use `Продовження таблиці __`

---

## 10. Formulas and Equations

- Centered on the line, inline with surrounding text
- At least **one blank line** above and below each formula
- **Number format:** `(3.1)` — parentheses, placed at the **right margin** of the same line
- Symbol explanations: immediately after the formula, first line starts from indent with word **`де`** (no colon), each symbol on a new line
- Example:
  ```
  z = (m₁ − m₂) / √(d₁² + d₂²),                    (7.1)

  де m₁, m₂ – математичне очікування; d₁, d₂ – середнє квадратичне відхилення.
  ```
- If a formula wraps to the next line, break at **operation signs** (repeat the sign at the start of the next line); for multiplication use `×`
- Consecutive formulas not separated by text are separated by a comma

---

## 11. In-Text Citations

- Format: **`[number]`** or **`[range]`** in square brackets — e.g., `[1–4]`, `[7]`
- References to sections/figures/tables use their numbers: `у розділі 4`, `рис. 1.3`, `табл. 3.2`, `формула (3.1)`, `додаток Б`

---

## 12. List of Abbreviations / Symbols

- Alphabetical order (Ukrainian alphabet first, then Latin)
- Two-column layout: **left** — abbreviation/symbol, **right** — full explanation
- On first occurrence in text, write the full term; on subsequent occurrences, the abbreviation alone

---

## 13. Appendices

- Ordered by **order of first reference** in the text
- Each appendix starts on a **new page**
- Label format: centered, small letters — `Додаток А` (first letter capital)
- Below that, centered — the appendix title
- Labeled sequentially: А, Б, В, Г, … (Ukrainian capital letters)
- Internal numbering of figures/tables/formulas: within the appendix — e.g., `рисунок А.2`, `таблиця Б.3`, `формула (В.1)`
- Internal sections: `А.2` = section 2 of Appendix А; `Г.3.1` = subsection 3.1 of Appendix Г
- Appendices share **continuous page numbering** with the rest of the document
- Graphic materials (drawings, diagrams) executed on computer: A4 format

---

## 14. References Format (ДСТУ 8302:2015)

### Book — one author
`Прізвище І. Б. Назва книги : тип видання. Місто : Видавництво, Рік. Кількість с.`

### Book — two authors
`Прізвище І. Б., Прізвище І. Б. Назва. Місто : Видавництво, Рік. Кількість с.`

### Book — four or more authors
`Прізвище І. Б. та ін. Назва / за ред. І. Б. Прізвище. Місто : Видавництво, Рік. Кількість с.`

### Journal article
`Прізвище І. Б. Назва статті. Назва журналу. Рік. № X. С. XX–XX.`

### Conference proceedings
`Прізвище І. Б. Назва тез. Назва конференції : матеріали конф. Місто, Рік. С. XX–XX.`

### Electronic resource
`Прізвище І. Б. Назва. Назва сайту. URL: http://... (дата звернення: ДД.ММ.РРРР).`

### Dissertation
`Прізвище І. Б. Назва : дис. ... канд./д-ра ... наук : XX.XX.XX. Місто, Рік. Кількість с.`

### Dissertation abstract (автореферат)
`Прізвище І. Б. Назва : автореф. дис. ... канд. ... наук : XX.XX.XX. Місто, Рік. Кількість с.`

---

## 15. Annotation

- Both **Ukrainian** and **English** versions required
- Max **250 words** per language
- Must reflect the main content of the work

---

## 16. Defense Presentation

- Duration: **up to 10 minutes**
- Language: same as the written work
- Must be accompanied by **multimedia presentation (slides)**
- Four parts:
  1. **Introduction** (~1 min) — relevance, problem statement
  2. **Research task** (~3 min) — object, subject, goals, tasks, methods
  3. **Main results** (~5 min) — methods used, key findings, algorithms/programs/results
  4. **Conclusions** (~1 min) — application areas, publications, recommendations

---

## 17. Grading (100 points total)

| Grader | Points |
|---|---|
| Supervisor | 30 |
| Reviewer | 20 |
| Examination commission (per member × 10 pts each) | 50 |

Commission breakdown per member (10 pts):
- Formatting: 2 pts
- Presentation quality: 6 pts
- Q&A answers: 2 pts

---

## 18. Submission Timeline

| Step | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Topic approved at department + faculty council | ~6 months before defense |
| Reviewer list approved at department | no later than 1 month before |
| Pre-defense at department + submit PDF to department | no later than 2 weeks before |
| Plagiarism check (PDF submission) | no later than 12 days before |
| Submit 2 hard copies (hardcover) + PDF + supervisor's review | 7 days before |
| Reviewer submits written review | within 4 days, but no later than 3 days before |
| Secretary submits one copy to dean's office | no later than 2 days before |

---

## 19. Academic Integrity

- Submit PDF for **plagiarism check** (anti-plagiarism system)
- Negative review from external reviewer is **not** grounds for rejection — defense still proceeds
- Supervisor's review covers: independence, correspondence to assignment, main achievements/shortcomings, research elements, reasoning quality

---

## 20. Quick Checklist Before Submission

- [ ] Title page matches Appendix A template exactly
- [ ] Assignment signed by supervisor, filled from both sides (Appendix B)
- [ ] Annotation in Ukrainian and English, ≤250 words each
- [ ] Table of contents lists all sections with page numbers
- [ ] Introduction covers all 7 required items (relevance → future directions)
- [ ] Literature review ≤20% of main body
- [ ] Main body 50–60 pages
- [ ] Every figure has caption below; every table has caption above
- [ ] All figures/tables/formulas are cited in text
- [ ] Formulas numbered `(chapter.number)` at right margin
- [ ] All citations in `[n]` format with matching bibliography entry
- [ ] Bibliography formatted per ДСТУ 8302:2015
- [ ] Appendices labeled А, Б, В…; each on new page
- [ ] Page numbers top-right, title/annotation/contents pages counted but unnumbered
- [ ] Font Times New Roman 14pt, 1.5 spacing, margins 30/20/20/10 mm
- [ ] PDF version prepared for plagiarism check
